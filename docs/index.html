Live settlement — an agent buying the service

Why this exists

The agent-commerce infrastructure is real. The seller economy is not, yet: median x402 seller revenue is ~$0.06/month, 38% earn nothing, top 10 sellers take ~98.5% of volume. So x402 here is a capability, not a business model. Real revenue must come from services and grants while the rails mature.
