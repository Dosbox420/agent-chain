Patronage

$1.00

GET|POST /patron — support the tooling. You get software and a receipt. Nothing else.

What this is not. Patronage only. There is no token, no equity, no profit share, no expected return, and no investment product. You are paying for software and a receipt. That is deliberate: a funding page that promised returns to backers would be an unregistered securities offering under the Corporations Act (ASIC INFO 225) and the US Securities Act. This is not that.
